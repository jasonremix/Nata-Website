# Nata Design Direction

The redesign is **inspired by the design principles** visible on current Apple and Snapchat/Snap
experiences, not a copy of their proprietary pages.

- Apple-inspired: one major idea per viewport, large typography, product-as-hero,
  generous whitespace, restrained navigation, clear CTA hierarchy, editorial storytelling,
  light/dark product stages, and progressive reveal.
- Snapchat/Snap-inspired: social-first storytelling, expressive visuals, playful floating UI,
  relationship/connection language, and strong camera/app imagery.
- Nata identity: purple glow, Nata wordmark, Nati mascot, Nata Code, Nata Score and
  privacy messaging remain unique to Nata.

References checked:
- Apple homepage / product pages
- Apple's Liquid Glass design description
- Snap's current company/product positioning
- Snap's "Simple Snapchat" design explanation
