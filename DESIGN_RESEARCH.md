# Nata Ultra — Design research

This version translates current product-site principles into a unique Nata identity.

Observed principles:
- Apple: current homepage uses product-led hero blocks, very large headlines, short copy,
  clear "Learn more / Buy" style actions, strong product imagery and alternating visual stages.
  Apple's current software direction also emphasizes content-first layouts, dynamic translucency,
  readability and consistent navigation.
- Snapchat/Snap: camera/social-first storytelling, direct expression, relationships, vertical/full-screen
  visual communication and simple navigation.
- Airbnb/other premium product sites: task clarity, strong category hierarchy and progressive discovery.

Nata adds its own visual language: purple/lavender, Nata Code, Nata Score, Nati and a privacy-first story.
No proprietary Apple/Snap markup, logos, copy or assets are reproduced.
